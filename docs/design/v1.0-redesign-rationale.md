# Why v1.0 is redesigned: the v0.3 learning curve and how each cause is fixed

Owner: design · 2026-11-02 · Companion to `docs/design/GDD.md` (v1.0-draft).

Eddie, a veteran MOBA player, needed about **3 matches** to understand v0.3. The concepts were familiar; the
*translation* was not. Below are the specific v0.3 rules (from `prototype-web/src/engine.js`) that a veteran has to
learn on top of what they already know, and the v1.0 fix for each.

## 1. The action budget had three hidden layers
v0.3: each turn a hero has MOV tiles that can be split around **one** action (`u.acted`), but some abilities are
`quick` (free: Battle Cry, Smoke Veil, Frost Shot, Tumble, Enchant Totem), item actives are free, potions are free,
and Frostbow's first basic attack is free (`freeAttack`/`freeUsed`). Which buttons end your options is learned by trial.
**Fix:** 3 actions, each once (Move twice). Recall and Shop are the only free things. Frostbow's extra attack becomes
"your Attack hits twice", so it lives inside the same budget (from Skytear's 3 AP / no-repeat rule).

## 2. Minions were 30+ individual units with their own AI
v0.3 `minionAct`/`minionTarget`: each minion paths 2 tiles, picks a target by a 4-step priority (hero who hit a hero
within 2 → nearest minion → nearest structure → nearest anything), deals +2 vs minions and +2 + round/8 vs structures,
and gains HP/ATK every 10 rounds. Players could not predict who would win a lane, only watch the world phase.
**Fix:** a lane is three numbers (Front, Azure wave, Ember wave). Power = minions + heroes near the Front; more Power wins
and pushes. A forecast arrow shows the result before it happens. Minion minis stay for the look and for last hits.

## 3. Tower aggro was a memory rule
v0.3 `structAct`: a tower prefers a hero whose `aggro === G.round` (damaged an enemy hero *this round*), then the nearest
minion, then anyone; damage is 7 + round/6 vs heroes but 4 vs minions; tier-2 towers are invulnerable while tier-1 stands;
the Core is invulnerable while every lane has a tower. Correct play required remembering who hit whom this round.
**Fix:** one sentence: "hurt a hero under their tower and it shoots you for 3; stand under it without minions and it shoots
you in the Lane phase." Heroes can only damage towers with their own wave there (no separate invulnerability rules).

## 4. Whole-team phases made you wait, then react to nothing
v0.3 `progress`: all of Azure acts, then all of Ember, then the world phase simulates every tower, minion and neutral.
At 4v4 you watch 4 enemy turns plus the world before acting again, and you cannot respond to an enemy's dive until
the damage is done. **Fix:** alternating activations (from Skytear), with **duo turns** at 3v3/4v4 to keep the wall clock
down (sim: 4v4 Rift 42–58 min with duo turns vs 72–109 with single activations).

## 5. Hidden progression: XP thresholds, scaling stats, scaling neutrals
v0.3: XP table 0/3/7/12/18/25 fed by minion deaths within 3 tiles, kills (3 + victim level), assists, towers, camps and
objectives; per-hero growth rates (`g: {hp: 5, atk: .5, pwr: .5}`) with fractional stats floored; neutrals gain +1 HP per
round; respawn = 1 + ceil(lvl/2) + floor(round/15). Nobody could say when their ultimate would arrive.
**Fix:** everyone levels at fixed rounds (L3 = ultimate at round 5), same gains for every hero, fixed neutral HP,
respawn = skip one activation (from Skytear's mana that grows each turn).

## 6. Two damage types, two defences, crits and randomness
v0.3: physical vs magic (`mag`), ARM vs RES, true damage (burn, towers, thorns), 30% crits, Omnislash picks random
targets, `Math.random()` unseeded. Hard to predict, impossible to replay. **Fix:** armor blocks Attacks only; abilities
ignore it; no dice, no crits. Fully deterministic given a seed, which also serves online sync and bots.

## 7. A 17-item shop with 6 slots
v0.3: prices 50–1800, potions with a carry limit, boots group rules, upgrade discounts, unique items, selling at half.
**Fix:** 14 items, 3 slots, two prices (basic 3, upgrade 7 or 4), no selling, no potions (base heals fully, as Eddie asked),
3 recommended items per hero.

## 8. No natural ending
v0.3 bot games stalled; overtime made both Cores crumble 4 HP/round from round 25 (16 on Frost Bridge). In 30 bot games
I re-ran on the frozen engine, **26 reached overtime** (rounds 13–39). Overtime is a timer, not a goal.
**Fix:** a VP race visible from round 1 (hero KO 1, tower 3, Drake 2, Golem/Queen 3; target 6–12 by team size) plus a
round limit (12; Frost Bridge 8), from Rum & Bones' 6-VP race and Skytear's short game. Sim: every match ends by round 12;
Cores still fall in 43–60% of Rift games, so "break the Core" stays the dramatic win.

## 9. Square grid with diagonals
v0.3: Chebyshev distance on 8 directions; lines in 8 directions (Meat Hook's tooltip: "Diagonals count too").
**Fix:** hexes: 6 directions, one distance, readable on a 3D table (Skytear standard).

## What we deliberately kept
Last hits for gold, the 8 heroes and their ability identities, Drake/Golem/Spider Queen and altars, the three maps and their
names, recall at any point (home next turn, cancelled by damage), full heal in base, no recall + relics on Frost Bridge,
and an extra attack for the marksman.

## Risk
Over-abstraction could lose the MOBA feel (research note risk #1). Mitigations: minion minis still march to the Front
and die to your last hits, towers still punish dives, the jungle and objective steals remain. The main contested choices
are listed for Eddie in GDD section 17.
