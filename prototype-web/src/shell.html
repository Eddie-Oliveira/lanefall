Lanefall

A turn-based MOBA you can play at the kitchen table. Three maps: the three-lane Rift, the two-lane Twin Hollow, and the one-lane Frost Bridge brawl. Towers, minion waves, a jungle and a shop. Duel one-on-one or bring a warband of four. Bots fill the empty seats.
